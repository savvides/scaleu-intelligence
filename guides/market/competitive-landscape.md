# Edtech competitive landscape

Many incumbents in K-12, higher ed, and corporate learning changed owners between 2022 and 2026, and the general AI platforms now sell directly to schools and universities. This page names who sells in each segment and who owns them, so you can place your product against what a buyer already licenses.

**Applies to:** K-12, higher education, and corporate learning and development (L&D), United States.

## How to use this page

Use it to name incumbents in a pitch, find integration partners, and check whether a competitor has been acquired, merged, or restructured. Ownership facts link to a company announcement or filing. The short positioning notes and the lists of what wins and what fails are practitioner judgment from the original Founder Stack authors, not market-share data. Company status changes quickly and this page is meant to be updated quarterly, so open the linked source before you cite a fact in sales or fundraising materials. Items the last check could not confirm are listed in the footer.

## K-12

### Learning management and course delivery

- **Google Classroom**: free, integrated with Google Workspace for Education
- **Canvas (Instructure)**: also used by districts that want more than Google Classroom
- **Schoology (PowerSchool)**: integrated with the PowerSchool SIS
- **Seesaw**: portfolio-based, used mainly in elementary grades

### Assessment and testing

- **Renaissance (Star Assessments)**: benchmark and progress monitoring. Renaissance also owns [Illuminate Education](https://www.k12dive.com/news/following-high-profile-data-breach-illuminate-education-acquired-by-ed-tec/630927/) (2022), which provides assessment management and data analytics.
- **NWEA (MAP Growth)**: adaptive assessments for growth measurement; [a division of HMH](https://www.hmhco.com/hmh-and-nwea) since 2023
- **Curriculum Associates (i-Ready)**: diagnostic and instruction

### Adaptive learning and tutoring

- **Khan Academy (Khanmigo)**: nonprofit; Khanmigo is [free for teachers](https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing)
- **IXL Learning**: adaptive practice across subjects; [acquired Evan-Moor](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ixl-learning-acquires-evan-moor-educational-publishers-302573968.html) and MyTutor in 2025
- **DreamBox (Discovery Education)**: adaptive math; [acquired by Discovery Education](https://discoveryeducation.com/details/clearlake-capital-backed-discovery-education-completes-acquisition-of-dreambox-learning/) in 2023
- **Zearn**: nonprofit math curriculum with a free core product

### Student information systems

- **PowerSchool**: [taken private by Bain Capital](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835681/000119312524230179/d94063dex991.htm) in October 2024. A December 2024 [breach of its systems](https://www.powerschool.com/security/sis-incident/notice-of-united-states-data-breach/) exposed student and staff data, and districts now ask vendors about it.
- **Infinite Campus**
- **Skyward**

### AI-specific (emerging, 2025-2026)

- **Khanmigo (Khan Academy)**: AI tutoring and teacher assistant
- **MagicSchool**: AI tools for teachers (lesson planning, assessments, feedback); [raised a $45M Series B](https://www.magicschool.ai/blog-posts/series-b-fundraise-for-teacher-ai) in 2025
- **SchoolAI**: AI tools for student interaction; [raised a $25M Series A](https://www.k12dive.com/press-release/20250401-schoolai-secures-25-million-to-help-teachers-and-schools-reach-every-stude/) in 2025
- **Diffit**: AI-generated differentiated materials for teachers. Its [site](https://web.diffit.me/) was offering the product on November 11, 2026; ownership and funding were not verified.
- **Gemini for Education (Google)**: [included in Google Workspace for Education](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/gemini-iste-2025/) since June 2025
- **Claude for Teachers (Anthropic)**: [free for verified US K-12 teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers), announced July 2026

### What wins and what fails in K-12

Practitioner view.

| Winners have | Common failures |
|---|---|
| Integration with the systems a district already runs (Google, PowerSchool, Canvas) | Building for administrators when teachers are the users |
| Evidence of effectiveness ([ESSA tiers](../evidence/evidence-tiers.md) matter in procurement) | Ignoring the procurement cycle and expecting fast sales |
| Fit with a teacher's workflow: time saved on a specific task | No FERPA-compliant data governance story (see [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md)) |
| A free tier for teachers, which allows bottom-up adoption | Over-engineering when teachers want simplicity |
| Alignment to state standards and Common Core | Treating all districts alike (rural and urban, Title I and affluent) |

For the market itself, see the [K-12 landscape](k12-landscape.md).

## Higher education

### LMS and course delivery

- **Canvas (Instructure)**: Instructure was [taken private by KKR and Dragoneer](https://www.instructure.com/press-release/kkr-and-dragoneer-complete-acquisition-instructure) in November 2024
- **Blackboard**: Anthology, Blackboard's parent, filed for Chapter 11 in September 2025 and sold its other units. It [emerged as Blackboard](https://blackboard.com/news/blackboard-formerly-anthology-emerges-debt-free-and-focused) on March 2, 2026. The announcement thanks its financial sponsors without naming them.
- **D2L Brightspace**: used by many community colleges and Canadian institutions. In April 2026 D2L [received an unsolicited take-private proposal](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/d2l-inc-responds-media-report-163800449.html). D2L's [newsroom](https://www.d2l.com/newsroom/) listed no transaction through November 10, 2026; the outcome was not verified.
- **Moodle**: open source, widely used internationally

### Student information systems and ERP

- **Ellucian**: [acquired Anthology's SIS and ERP business](https://www.ellucian.com/en-gb/newsroom/ellucian-completes-acquisition-anthologys-sis-and-erp-business) (closed December 31, 2025)

### Student success and retention

- **EAB Navigate**: advising and student success platform
- **Starfish (EAB)**: early alert and intervention; EAB acquired it from Hobsons in 2021
- **Civitas Learning**: predictive analytics for retention; [independent](https://www.civitaslearning.com/), not part of Anthology
- **Encoura**: [acquired Anthology's CRM and Student Success units](https://encoura.org/anthology) (closed February 2026)
- **Degree Works (Ellucian)**: degree audit and planning

### Online program management (OPM)

- **Risepoint** (formerly Academic Partnerships): first by share of active partnerships in [Validated Insights' 2025 OPM data](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251030532249/en/New-Validated-Insights-Report-OPM-Market-Continues-Slowdown). [Acquired Keypath Education's North American operations](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/risepoint-acquires-keypath-educations-north-american-operations-to-expand-online-healthcare-education-and-clinical-placement-services-302854011.html) in August 2026.
- **2U (edX)**: [emerged from Chapter 11 as a private company](https://2u.com/newsroom/2u-successfully-completes-transaction/) in September 2024
- **Coursera**: platform plus degree partnerships
- **Noodle**: unbundled OPM services

### Assessment

- **Turnitin**: plagiarism detection; [AI writing detection since April 2023](https://www.turnitin.com/press/turnitin-turns-on-ai-writing-detection-capabilities-for-educators-and-institutions)
- **Gradescope (Turnitin)**: AI-assisted grading
- **Proctorio** and **Meazure Learning** (ProctorU): online proctoring. ProctorU [rebranded as Meazure Learning](https://www.meazurelearning.com/resources/proctoru-and-yardstick-rebrand-to-meazure-learning) in 2021 and [acquired Examity](https://www.meazurelearning.com/resources/meazure-learning-strengthens-position-as-global-leader-of-assessment-solutions-with-acquisition-of-examity) in 2023.
- **Watermark Insights**: assessment and accreditation software. Main Capital Partners, a private equity firm, [announced its intent to acquire it](https://main.nl/press-release/main-capital-partners-announces-intent-to-acquire-watermark-insights/) on September 16, 2026. The release was unchanged on November 11, 2026; closing was not verified.

### AI in higher ed (emerging, 2025-2026)

- **ChatGPT Edu (OpenAI)**: [institutional ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-edu/), launched May 2024
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot for education**: [academic offering](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/blog/2025/10/designing-microsoft-365-copilot-to-empower-educators-students-and-staff/) announced October 2025
- **Gemini for Education (Google)**: [launched June 2025](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/gemini-iste-2025/)
- **Claude for Education (Anthropic)**: [launched April 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education)
- **AI tutoring and writing assistants**: many entrants, changing quickly, with no clear leader

Startups crowd here too. Of the 88 higher-ed and workforce companies ScaleU sourced in July–September 2026, 17 built AI grading and integrity tools and 17 built AI tutors, study tools, or course assistants ([field note](../../sources/notes/2026-10-30-scaleu-q1-fy27-update.md); ScaleU pipeline data, not a market count). The [higher-ed jobs atlas](../demand/higher-ed-jobs-atlas.md) shows which phases of the student journey are crowded.

### What wins and what fails in higher ed

Practitioner view.

| Winners have | Common failures |
|---|---|
| LTI 1.3 integration, with Canvas first | No LTI integration |
| Accessibility compliance, including a VPAT (see [higher-ed landscape](highered-landscape.md#accessibility-in-higher-ed)) | Ignoring accessibility until procurement |
| A passed security review | Underestimating how long security review takes |
| Evidence from peer institutions | Building for one institution's workflow and assuming it generalizes |
| Faculty champions | Selling top-down without faculty support |

### What wins and what fails in corporate L&D

Practitioner view.

| Winners have | Common failures |
|---|---|
| A SOC 2 Type II report | No SOC 2 when selling to enterprises |
| Integration with the HR system and the LMS | Content without distribution through the LMS |
| Reporting for managers | Attention to the learner's experience and none to manager and admin reporting |
| A measurable business result | No customer logos or case studies |
| A mobile-first experience | Pricing too low for enterprise buyers, who read it as a lack of seriousness |

See [corporate learning and development](corporate-ld.md) for buyers, budgets, and security review.

## Cross-segment trends (2025-2026)

1. **General AI platforms sell direct.** OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, and Anthropic now sell to schools and universities. The question for a startup is how its product differs from what the institution already licenses (interpretation; see [defensibility](../product/defensibility-moats.md) and the [AI-native framework](../product/ai-native-framework.md)).
2. **Ownership keeps changing.** Private-equity take-privates (PowerSchool, Instructure), restructurings (2U, Anthology and Blackboard), and mergers (Coursera and Udemy) reshaped incumbents in 2024–2026. In higher ed, Risepoint bought Keypath's North American operations in August 2026, and Main Capital Partners announced its intent to acquire Watermark Insights in September 2026. A change of owner can open a gap for a startup. It also creates platform risk when the owner of a partner changes. For who buys edtech companies, see [exits and acquirers](../business/exits-and-acquirers.md).
3. **Buyers ask for evidence (practitioner observation).** They increasingly want more than testimonials. See [evidence tiers](../evidence/evidence-tiers.md).
4. **Privacy rules are tightening.** The amended COPPA Rule took full effect in April 2026, and state laws keep changing. See [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md).
5. **Accessibility is a legal requirement.** The ADA Title II web rule makes WCAG 2.1 AA mandatory for public schools and universities, with compliance dates in 2027 and 2028. See [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md#accessibility-requirements).
