# EdTech Competitive Landscape

**Answers:** Who already sells into my segment, and who owns them now? Use this guide to name incumbents in a pitch, find integration partners, or check whether a competitor has been acquired, merged, or restructured.

**Audience:** K-12, higher education, and corporate L&D, United States. Ownership facts link to a company announcement or filing. The short positioning notes ("strong in elementary", "mid-market") are practitioner judgment from the original Founder Stack authors, not market-share data. This file is meant to be updated quarterly, because company status changes quickly; check the linked source before you cite a fact in sales or fundraising materials.

## K-12

### Learning Management / Course Delivery
- **Google Classroom**: free, integrated with Google Workspace for Education
- **Canvas (Instructure)**: also used by districts that want more than Google Classroom
- **Schoology (PowerSchool)**: integrated with the PowerSchool SIS
- **Seesaw**: portfolio-based, used mainly in elementary grades

### Assessment & Testing
- **Renaissance (Star Assessments)**: benchmark and progress monitoring. Renaissance also owns [Illuminate Education](https://www.k12dive.com/news/following-high-profile-data-breach-illuminate-education-acquired-by-ed-tec/630927/) (2022), which provides assessment management and data analytics.
- **NWEA (MAP Growth)**: adaptive assessments for growth measurement; [a division of HMH](https://www.hmhco.com/hmh-and-nwea) since 2023
- **Curriculum Associates (i-Ready)**: diagnostic and instruction

### Adaptive Learning / Tutoring
- **Khan Academy (Khanmigo)**: nonprofit; Khanmigo is [free for teachers](https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing)
- **IXL Learning**: adaptive practice across subjects; [acquired Evan-Moor](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ixl-learning-acquires-evan-moor-educational-publishers-302573968.html) and MyTutor in 2025
- **DreamBox (Discovery Education)**: adaptive math; [acquired by Discovery Education](https://discoveryeducation.com/details/clearlake-capital-backed-discovery-education-completes-acquisition-of-dreambox-learning/) in 2023
- **Zearn**: nonprofit math curriculum with a free core product

### Student Information Systems (SIS)
- **PowerSchool**: [taken private by Bain Capital](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835681/000119312524230179/d94063dex991.htm) in October 2024. A December 2024 [breach of its systems](https://www.powerschool.com/security/sis-incident/notice-of-united-states-data-breach/) exposed student and staff data, and districts now ask vendors about it.
- **Infinite Campus**
- **Skyward**

### AI-Specific (emerging, 2025-2026)
- **Khanmigo (Khan Academy)**: AI tutoring and teacher assistant
- **MagicSchool**: AI tools for teachers (lesson planning, assessments, feedback); [raised a $45M Series B](https://www.magicschool.ai/blog-posts/series-b-fundraise-for-teacher-ai) in 2025
- **SchoolAI**: AI tools for student interaction; [raised a $25M Series A](https://www.k12dive.com/press-release/20250401-schoolai-secures-25-million-to-help-teachers-and-schools-reach-every-stude/) in 2025
- **Diffit**: AI-generated differentiated materials for teachers (current status not verified)
- **Gemini for Education (Google)**: [included in Google Workspace for Education](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/gemini-iste-2025/) since June 2025
- **Claude for Teachers (Anthropic)**: [free for verified US K-12 teachers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers), announced July 2026

### What differentiates winners in K-12 (practitioner view)
- Integration with existing ecosystems (Google, PowerSchool, Canvas)
- Evidence of effectiveness ([ESSA tiers](../evidence/evidence-tiers.md) matter for procurement)
- Teacher workflow fit (saves time on specific tasks)
- Free or freemium tier for teachers (bottom-up adoption)
- Standards alignment (state standards, Common Core)

### Common failure modes in K-12 (practitioner view)
- Building for administrators when teachers are the users
- Ignoring the procurement cycle and expecting fast sales
- Not having a FERPA-compliant data governance story (see [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md))
- Over-engineering when teachers want simplicity
- Assuming all districts are the same (rural vs. urban, Title I vs. affluent)

## Higher Education

### LMS / Course Delivery
- **Canvas (Instructure)**: Instructure was [taken private by KKR and Dragoneer](https://www.instructure.com/press-release/kkr-and-dragoneer-complete-acquisition-instructure) in November 2024
- **Blackboard**: Anthology, Blackboard's parent, filed for Chapter 11 in September 2025 and sold its other units. It [emerged as Blackboard](https://blackboard.com/news/blackboard-formerly-anthology-emerges-debt-free-and-focused) on March 2, 2026.
- **D2L Brightspace**: used by many community colleges and Canadian institutions. In April 2026 D2L [received an unsolicited take-private proposal](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/d2l-inc-responds-media-report-163800449.html); the outcome was not verified.
- **Moodle**: open source, widely used internationally

### Student Information Systems / ERP
- **Ellucian**: [acquired Anthology's SIS and ERP business](https://www.ellucian.com/en-gb/newsroom/ellucian-completes-acquisition-anthologys-sis-and-erp-business) (closed December 31, 2025)

### Student Success / Retention
- **EAB Navigate**: advising and student success platform
- **Starfish (EAB)**: early alert and intervention; EAB acquired it from Hobsons in 2021
- **Civitas Learning**: predictive analytics for retention; [independent](https://www.civitaslearning.com/), not part of Anthology
- **Encoura**: [acquired Anthology's CRM and Student Success units](https://encoura.org/anthology) (closed February 2026)
- **Degree Works (Ellucian)**: degree audit and planning

### Online Program Management (OPM)
- **Risepoint** (formerly Academic Partnerships): first by share of active partnerships in [Validated Insights' 2025 OPM data](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251030532249/en/New-Validated-Insights-Report-OPM-Market-Continues-Slowdown). [Acquired Keypath Education's North American operations](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/risepoint-acquires-keypath-educations-north-american-operations-to-expand-online-healthcare-education-and-clinical-placement-services-302854011.html) in August 2026.
- **2U (edX)**: [emerged from Chapter 11 as a private company](https://2u.com/newsroom/2u-successfully-completes-transaction/) in September 2024
- **Coursera**: platform plus degree partnerships
- **Noodle**: unbundled OPM services

### Assessment
- **Turnitin**: plagiarism detection; [AI writing detection since April 2023](https://www.turnitin.com/press/turnitin-turns-on-ai-writing-detection-capabilities-for-educators-and-institutions)
- **Gradescope (Turnitin)**: AI-assisted grading
- **Proctorio** and **Meazure Learning** (ProctorU): online proctoring. ProctorU [rebranded as Meazure Learning](https://www.meazurelearning.com/resources/proctoru-and-yardstick-rebrand-to-meazure-learning) in 2021 and [acquired Examity](https://www.meazurelearning.com/resources/meazure-learning-strengthens-position-as-global-leader-of-assessment-solutions-with-acquisition-of-examity) in 2023.

### AI-Specific (emerging, 2025-2026)
- **ChatGPT Edu (OpenAI)**: [institutional ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-edu/), launched May 2024
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot for education**: [academic offering](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/blog/2025/10/designing-microsoft-365-copilot-to-empower-educators-students-and-staff/) announced October 2025
- **Gemini for Education (Google)**: [launched June 2025](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/gemini-iste-2025/)
- **Claude for Education (Anthropic)**: [launched April 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education)
- **Various AI tutoring and writing assistants**: rapidly evolving, with no clear leader

Startups crowd here too. Of the 88 higher-ed and workforce companies ScaleU sourced in July–September 2026, 17 built AI grading and integrity tools and 17 built AI tutors, study tools, or course assistants ([field note](../../sources/notes/2026-10-30-scaleu-q1-fy27-update.md); ScaleU pipeline data, not a market count).

### What differentiates winners in higher ed (practitioner view)
- LTI 1.3 integration (Canvas especially)
- Accessibility compliance (VPAT required; see [higher-ed landscape](highered-landscape.md#accessibility-in-higher-ed))
- Passing a rigorous security review
- Evidence from peer institutions
- Faculty champion development

### Common failure modes in higher ed (practitioner view)
- Selling top-down without faculty buy-in
- Ignoring accessibility requirements until procurement
- Underestimating security review timelines
- Building for one institution's workflow and assuming it generalizes
- Not supporting LTI integration

- **Cornerstone OnDemand**: owned by Clearlake since 2021
- **SAP SuccessFactors Learning**: integrated with SAP HR
- **Workday Learning**: integrated with Workday HCM
- **Docebo**: AI-focused LMS; Intercap [became majority holder](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260911705959/en/Docebo-Inc.-Announces-Final-Results-of-its-Substantial-Issuer-Bid) in 2026
- **Absorb LMS**: mid-market

### Learning Experience Platforms (LXP)
- **Degreed**: skills intelligence plus LXP
- **EdCast**: [acquired by Cornerstone](https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/company/news-room/press-releases/clearlake-capital-backed-cornerstone-completes-acquisition-of-edcast/) in 2022
- **360Learning**

### Content / Skills Platforms
- **LinkedIn Learning**: large professional-skills library
- **Coursera (including Udemy)**: Coursera [completed its merger with Udemy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1651562/000114036126020399/ef20072971_8k.htm) on May 11, 2026. Coursera for Business and Udemy Business now belong to one company.
- **Pluralsight**: technical skills; [lenders took control from Vista](https://goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2024/08/alerts-privateequity-ma-pluralsight-change-of-control-transaction) in August 2024
- **Skillsoft**: public; [agreed in May 2026 to sell Global Knowledge](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260520936193/en/) (closing not verified)

### Compliance Training
- **SAI360**: compliance and risk management
- **NAVEX**: ethics and compliance training; a [Goldman Sachs Alternatives-led group took a majority stake](https://www.bcpartners.com/news/a-consortium-led-by-goldman-sachs-alternatives-including-blackstone-completes-majority-stake-acquisition-of-navex/) in October 2025
- **Traliant**: compliance training (harassment, DEI)

### What differentiates winners in corporate L&D (practitioner view)
- Measurable ROI / business impact
- HRIS/LMS integration
- SOC 2 Type II certification
- Mobile-first experience
- Manager visibility and reporting

### Common failure modes in corporate L&D (practitioner view)
- Not having SOC 2 when selling to enterprises
- Building content without distribution (LMS integration)
- Focusing on learner experience but not manager/admin reporting
- Pricing too low for enterprise (signals lack of seriousness)
- Not having customer logos / case studies

See [corporate L&D](corporate-ld.md) for buyers and procurement.

## Cross-segment trends (2025-2026)

1. **AI everywhere:** General AI platforms (OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Anthropic) now sell directly to schools and universities. The question is less whether AI applies to your product than how it is differentiated from what institutions already license (interpretation; see [defensibility](../product/defensibility-moats.md)).
2. **Ownership churn:** Private-equity take-privates (PowerSchool, Instructure), restructurings (2U, Anthology/Blackboard), and mergers (Coursera and Udemy) reshaped incumbents in 2024–2026. In higher ed, Risepoint bought Keypath's North American operations in August 2026, and Main Capital Partners, a private equity firm, [announced its intent to acquire Watermark Insights](https://main.nl/press-release/main-capital-partners-announces-intent-to-acquire-watermark-insights/) (assessment and accreditation software) in September 2026. That can open gaps for startups and also creates platform risk when a partner's owner changes.
3. **Evidence expectations rising (practitioner observation):** Buyers increasingly ask for evidence, not just testimonials. See [evidence tiers](../evidence/evidence-tiers.md).
4. **Privacy rules tightening:** The amended COPPA Rule took full effect in April 2026, and state laws keep changing. See [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md).
5. **Accessibility as a legal requirement:** The ADA Title II web rule makes WCAG 2.1 AA mandatory for public schools and universities, with compliance dates in 2027 and 2028. See [K-12 regulation](k12-regulatory.md#accessibility-requirements).
