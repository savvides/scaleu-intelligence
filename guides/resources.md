# Resources: where else to look

This short directory lists the outside sources worth an edtech founder's time: what each is good for, who should use it, and what it costs. ScaleU opened every link on November 11, 2026, and left out anything dead, dormant since 2024, or useless behind a paywall.

**Applies to:** K-12, higher ed, and workforce founders, mainly in the United States; UK and international entries are marked.

**Disclosure.** This library's maintainer runs ASU ScaleU, which appears in the accelerator table this page links to. Inclusion here is editorial judgment: nobody paid or asked to be listed, and a listing is not an endorsement. The "good for" notes are ScaleU's view; dates, prices, and program terms come from each linked page.

## If you read only five things

ScaleU's view of the minimum weekly reading, by segment:

| You sell to | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K-12 districts | [EdWeek Market Brief](https://marketbrief.edweek.org/) | Written for companies that sell to districts, which no general outlet is |
| Colleges and universities | [On EdTech](https://onedtech.philhillaa.com/) | The closest reading of the higher-ed technology market |
| Employers | [Josh Bersin](https://joshbersin.com/) | How HR and learning leaders think about what they buy |
| Anyone raising money | [The EdSheet](https://edsheet.whiteboardadvisors.com/) | Deals, acquisitions, and policy in one biweekly issue |
| Anyone building with AI | [Edtech Insiders](https://www.edtechinsiders.org/) | Weekly news and founder interviews, plus a market map of AI tools |

## News and analysis

**K-12**

- [EdWeek Market Brief](https://marketbrief.edweek.org/): district purchasing, funding, and policy reported for vendors, with purchasing alerts and briefings. Paid membership; the site publishes no price and asks you to request a demo. For any founder with a K-12 sales team.
- [EdSurge](https://www.edsurge.com/): free news on teaching, AI, and school operations, published as an editorially independent news site of the International Society for Transforming Education (formerly ISTE+ASCD). Good for learning what teachers and administrators worry about.
- [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/): free, donor-funded K-12 news and commentary, strong on state policy and school choice.
- [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/): free daily briefs on district policy and operations. Skim the newsletter.
- [The Hechinger Report](https://hechingerreport.org/): free nonprofit reporting, from early childhood through college, on what works and for whom.

**Higher ed**

- [On EdTech](https://onedtech.philhillaa.com/), by Phil Hill and Glenda Morgan: LMS market share, online program management, enrollment data, and policy. Several posts a week; some are free, and a paid tier (On EdTech+) carries the rest.
- [Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/): daily news. Much is free with an account; the site offered a subscription at $119 a year in November 2026.
- [The Chronicle of Higher Education](https://www.chronicle.com/): the trade's paper of record. Subscription; many campuses have a site license.
- [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/): free daily briefs from Informa TechTarget. Its [2027 conference list](https://www.highereddive.com/news/top-higher-education-conferences-2027/832196/) is a useful planning aid.
- [e-Literate](https://eliterate.us/), by Michael Feldstein: long essays on learning technology and AI. Irregular; the latest post when checked was dated June 29, 2026.
- [The Report by Class Central](https://www.classcentral.com/report/): the best free coverage of MOOCs and the listed online-learning companies (Coursera, Duolingo, Chegg). Read it if you sell courses to consumers.

**Workforce**

- [Work Shift](https://workshift.org/): a nonprofit newsroom on the links between education and work. Its weekly newsletter, The Job, covers Workforce Pell, apprenticeships, and nondegree credentials.
- [Josh Bersin](https://joshbersin.com/): analysis of corporate learning, talent, and HR technology. Articles and a podcast are free; research sits behind a membership.

**The business of edtech**

- [The EdSheet](https://edsheet.whiteboardadvisors.com/), by Matt Tower at Whiteboard Advisors: funding, venture deals, mergers, and policy, every two weeks. Volume 45 was dated November 9, 2026.
- [Edtech Insiders newsletter](https://edtechinsiders.substack.com/), by Alex Sarlin: interviews and essays, several a month; the page reports more than 20,000 subscribers.
- [HolonIQ](https://www.holoniq.com/): global funding totals, regional company lists, and quarterly AI-in-education notes. The notes are free; the data platform is paid. The site now carries QS branding. Compare its figures only with its own earlier figures (see the [funding landscape](market/funding-landscape.md)).

## Podcasts

- [Edtech Insiders](https://www.edtechinsiders.org/), with Ben Kornell and Alex Sarlin: a weekly news episode ("Week in Edtech") and founder and investor interviews. Follow this show if you follow only one.
- [Future U](https://www.futureupodcast.com/), with Jeff Selingo and Michael Horn: how university leaders think about cost, enrollment, and change. Every other Tuesday.
- [The Key](https://www.insidehighered.com/podcasts/the-key), from Inside Higher Ed: interviews with higher-ed newsmakers; episode 219 ran in November 2026.
- The EdSurge Podcast, listed with EdSurge's [news](https://www.edsurge.com/): K-12 classroom questions, useful for hearing how teachers talk.
- Josh Bersin's podcast, on [his site](https://joshbersin.com/): a weekly monologue on HR and learning technology for founders selling to employers.

## Research and evidence

- [What Works Clearinghouse](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/) (IES): reviews of studies against federal evidence standards. Search it before claiming an ESSA tier, and read its [handbooks](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Handbooks) (version 5.0) before designing a study. The newest of its 30 [practice guides](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuides) is dated December 2024, so check the date of whatever you cite.
- [Evidence for ESSA](https://www.evidenceforessa.org/): a searchable list of PK-12 programs rated against ESSA standards, run by the Center for Research and Reform in Education at Johns Hopkins. Districts use it to shop; find out how competitors are rated.
- [EEF Teaching and Learning Toolkit](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit) (UK): plain summaries of the evidence for common classroom approaches, each with an impact estimate in months of progress. The fastest way to learn whether your core mechanism has support.
- [The Science of Learning, second edition](https://www.deansforimpact.org/tools-and-resources/the-science-of-learning) (Deans for Impact, May 19, 2026): cognitive science for teachers in a few pages. Give it to every product hire.
- [ERIC](https://eric.ed.gov/): the federal index of education research, with free full text for many reports.
- [EdWorkingPapers](https://edworkingpapers.com/) (Annenberg Institute at Brown): new studies before journal publication. These are working papers; most have not been peer reviewed.
- [Stanford SCALE AI Hub research repository](https://scale.stanford.edu/ai/repository): academic studies of generative AI in preK-12, 1,754 entries when checked, last updated November 2026. Its summaries are AI-generated and human-reviewed; read the paper before you cite it.
- [The Learning Agency](https://the-learning-agency.com/): runs the Tools Competition and publishes guides on learning engineering and AI benchmarks for education tasks.
- [Digital Promise](https://digitalpromise.org/): research-practice partnerships and the [EdTech Quality Collaborative](https://digitalpromise.org/programs/edtech-quality-collaborative/).
- In this library: [learning science](product/learning-science.md), the [evidence roadmap](evidence/evidence-roadmap.md), and paper collections on [19 topics](../sources/research/README.md).

## Standards and trust bodies

These groups write the rules and checklists that buyers copy into RFPs. [Vendor review readiness](selling/vendor-review-readiness.md) explains which of their badges matter.

| Body | What it controls | When a founder needs it |
|---|---|---|
| [1EdTech](https://www.1edtech.org/) | LTI, OneRoster, QTI, Open Badges, CLR, CASE; the [TrustEd Apps](https://www.1edtech.org/program/trustedapps) vetting program | Before the first LMS or rostering integration (see [interoperability](product/interoperability.md)) |
| [A4L and the Student Data Privacy Consortium](https://privacy.a4l.org/) | The National Data Privacy Agreement and a registry of about 234,000 signed agreements | Before the first district contract |
| [EDUCAUSE](https://www.educause.edu/) | The [HECVAT](https://www.educause.edu/hecvat) security questionnaire (version 4.1.6 when checked), free to vendors | Before the first university security review |
| [CoSN](https://www.cosn.org/) | The association of district technology leaders | To meet K-12 CTOs and learn their priorities |
| [SETDA](https://www.setda.org/) | The association of state edtech directors; publishes an annual state trends report and a procurement guide | When state policy or a state contract shapes your market |
| [ISTE](https://iste.org/) | Educator technology standards and ISTELive. ISTE merged with ASCD in 2023, and in 2026 the combined body took the name International Society for Transforming Education ([ISTELive FAQ](https://conference.iste.org/2027/about/faq.php)) | For reach among teachers and instructional leaders |
| [EDSAFE AI Alliance](https://www.edsafeai.org/) | The SAFE Benchmarks Framework for AI in education; an industry council of 175+ companies. Led by InnovateEDU | When districts ask how your AI product was vetted |
| [TeachAI](https://www.teachai.org/) | An AI guidance toolkit that districts adapt into policy. Its newest resources were dated 2025 | To predict what a district AI policy will say |
| [Common Sense Privacy Program](https://privacy.commonsense.org/) | Public ratings of privacy policies | Now: you may already be rated |
| [Future of Privacy Forum](https://fpf.org/youth-policy-governance/) | Analysis of student and youth privacy law. It retired the Student Privacy Pledge in 2025 (see [vendor review readiness](selling/vendor-review-readiness.md)) | To track state privacy bills |
| [Project Unicorn](https://www.projectunicorn.org/) | A vendor pledge and rubric on data interoperability, also from InnovateEDU | When districts ask about data portability |

## Non-dilutive funding portals

The [funding landscape](market/funding-landscape.md) and [fundraising](business/fundraising.md) cover grant terms and the 2025–2026 changes to SBIR. Search for grants in these places.

- [SBIR.gov](https://www.sbir.gov/): every agency's open topics, plus a database of past awards. Search competitors' names to see who has won what.
- [ED/IES SBIR](https://ies.ed.gov/funding/research/programs/small-business-innovation-research-sbir): the edtech-specific program. Phase I is $250,000 over 9 months and Phase II is $1,000,000 over 2 years; solicitations come out annually and close about 60 days later.
- [NSF America's Seed Fund](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/): SBIR and STTR for technology with research risk. You submit a short Project Pitch first.
- [IES funding opportunities](https://ies.ed.gov/funding): research grants, including the FY 2027 From Seedlings to Scale program. Most suit a company only as a partner to a research team; read each request for applications for eligibility.
- [Grants.gov](https://www.grants.gov/): all federal discretionary grants. Most education grants go to districts, states, and colleges, so search it to find applicants who need a product partner.
- [Tools Competition](https://tools-competition.org/): see the table above.
- Foundations: priorities for Gates, Walton, Dell, and Arnold Ventures are in the [funding landscape](market/funding-landscape.md).

## Communities and job boards

- [EdSurge Jobs](https://jobsboard.edsurge.com/): an edtech job board, useful for hiring sales and curriculum staff.
- [The EdSheet](https://edsheet.whiteboardadvisors.com/): a "Jobs of the Week" series and a reader community alongside the newsletter.
- [HigherEdJobs](https://www.higheredjobs.com/): university staff postings. Read them as market research: a new job title signals a new budget line.
- [Edtech Insiders events](https://www.edtechinsiders.org/events): small gatherings at the major conferences, usually sold out.
- [StartEd](https://www.started.com/): peer circles of edtech CEOs by stage.
- EDUCAUSE's HECVAT Users Community Group, linked from the [HECVAT page](https://www.educause.edu/hecvat): where university security staff discuss the questionnaire you will fill out.

## Books and long reads

- *Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning*, Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III, and Mark A. McDaniel ([Harvard University Press](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674729018), 2014). Retrieval, spacing, and interleaving, told through cases.
- *Failure to Disrupt: Why Technology Alone Can't Transform Education*, Justin Reich ([Harvard University Press](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674278684); paperback 2022). Why MOOCs, adaptive tutors, and other waves fell short of their promises. Read it before writing your pitch.
- *How Learning Works: Eight Research-Based Principles for Smart Teaching*, second edition, Marsha C. Lovett and coauthors ([Jossey-Bass](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/How+Learning+Works%3A+Eight+Research-Based+Principles+for+Smart+Teaching%2C+2nd+Edition-p-9781119861690), 2023). The college-teaching counterpart to *Make It Stick*.
- *The Mom Test*, Rob Fitzpatrick ([author's site](https://www.momtestbook.com/)). How to interview customers without leading them. Pair it with [demand validation](demand/demand-validation.md).
- [The Science of Learning, second edition](https://www.deansforimpact.org/tools-and-resources/the-science-of-learning), Deans for Impact (2026). Short, free to download, and enough to stop the commonest product mistakes.

## Founder basics outside edtech

- [Y Combinator Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library): free essays and videos on every early-stage topic. Start here for anything not specific to education.
- [YC SAFE documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents): the standard post-money SAFE forms (valuation cap, discount, and uncapped MFN) and the pro rata side letter. YC has used the post-money form since 2018.
- [NVCA model legal documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/): free templates for a priced round. The core financing documents were updated between October 2025 and June 2026. Read them before your first term sheet, so that you know the terms.
- [Stripe Atlas guides](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas-guides): plain guides to incorporation, founder equity, taxes, and SaaS pricing.
- [First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/): free, long articles on hiring, management, and finding product-market fit.
- [Bessemer Atlas](https://www.bvp.com/atlas): roadmaps, memos, and cloud benchmarks. Use the benchmarks with the cautions in [unit economics](business/unit-economics.md): general SaaS numbers assume a sales calendar that schools do not keep.
- [a16z](https://a16z.com/): essays on AI products and pricing from a generalist firm that has backed edtech.
