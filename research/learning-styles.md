# Learning Styles and Instructional Matching

Research on learning-style frameworks, learner preferences, and the hypothesis that matching instruction to a stated learning style affects learning outcomes. The collection also includes related work on personalization and instructional design.

The takeaways summarize the cited papers' findings and interpretations. See [scope and interpretation](README.md#scope-and-interpretation).
