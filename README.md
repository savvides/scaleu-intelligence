# ScaleU Intelligence

Practical knowledge for edtech founders and the people who work with them: how education buyers decide, what makes a pilot useful, and what research says about learning.

Maintained by Philippos Savvides, who runs [ASU ScaleU](https://scaleu.org), Arizona State University's edtech accelerator. This is a growing working library drawn from conferences, research, and experience with education companies and institutions.

[Download a release](https://github.com/savvides/scaleu-intelligence/releases/latest) · [What's changed](CHANGELOG.md) · [Get help](.github/SUPPORT.md)

## Use it with your AI assistant

Everything here is plain Markdown. Nothing needs to be installed or run.

- **Point your assistant at [`guides/`](guides/README.md) first.** The guides answer founder questions directly and link to the evidence behind them.
- **Let it follow links into [`sources/`](sources/README.md) when it needs detail.** Sources hold the original research summaries, event reports, and transcripts. They are large; an assistant does not need to read them all.
- **Ask it to cite** the document, the underlying source, and its date, and to separate evidence from interpretation.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/savvides/scaleu-intelligence.git
```

## What's in the library

- **[Guides](guides/README.md):** answers organized by founder question: demand, selling, evidence, product, and market.
- **[Sources](sources/README.md):** the evidence underneath.
  - [Learning research](sources/research/README.md): 19 topic collections with paper citations and DOIs.
  - [Events](sources/events/README.md): ASU+GSV 2026 reports and transcripts, and the SXSW EDU 2026 higher-ed framework.
  - [Field notes](sources/notes/README.md): the home for future observations from meetings and other work. None published yet.
- **[About](about/provenance.md):** where the material came from, plus [licenses](LICENSE.md) and the [field-note template](about/templates/field-note.md). Not needed to use the library.

## Read the evidence in context

Research summaries point to papers; conference transcripts record what speakers said; frameworks offer practitioner judgment. A speaker's claim about a product is not an independently verified result. Dates in imported material describe the original publication or update, not a new fact check. Verify current regulations, company status, and program terms at their primary sources before acting on them.

## Cite and reuse

Use `main` for the latest edits or a [tagged release](https://github.com/savvides/scaleu-intelligence/releases) for a stable snapshot. Each release includes the complete library as ZIP and tar.gz downloads under **Assets**.

Cite the library with the version or commit you used:

> Savvides, P. (2026). *ScaleU Intelligence* (Version 1.0.0). https://github.com/savvides/scaleu-intelligence

Cite individual authors, speakers, and original papers when drawing on their work; the library citation does not replace those credits. Reuse follows the [license for the relevant collection](LICENSE.md): this repository combines MIT and CC BY 4.0 material.
