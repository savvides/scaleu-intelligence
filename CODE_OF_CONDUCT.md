# Code of conduct

This library welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, roles, and levels of experience. In issues, pull requests, and other project interactions:

- Discuss claims and evidence respectfully. Critique the work without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or publish someone else's private information.
- Credit others' work, disclose relevant commercial interests, and avoid unsolicited promotion.
- Respect decisions about publication permissions and confidential material.

Philippos Savvides maintains and moderates the repository. The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For harassment or abuse, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls on the relevant content or profile. Do not repeat private information in a public complaint.
